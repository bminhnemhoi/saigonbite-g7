Request pipeline – “Place order” through the layers

One HTTP request, seven steps, one database transaction. The numbers match the UC1 sequence diagram.

1
Browser

POST /checkout

Cart, address, voucher and payment method from the Bootstrap checkout page.

2
Presentation

CheckoutController

Checks the role, binds the view model, calls OrderService. No rules here.

3
Business

HubLoadService

Load = Confirmed + Preparing ÷ capacity. ≥ 90% → reject and offer other hubs.

4
Business

PricingService

BR1–BR12 in the BR10 order, rounded once. Returns the full price breakdown.

5
Business

Payment strategy

Resolver picks one of 5 strategies by customer type (BR13, BR14).

6
Data

One transaction

Order (Placed) + lines + price snapshot + history row + payment record.

7
Presentation

Tracking view

Confirmation and the live status timeline for the customer.

Hub ≥ 90% (step 3) – nothing is saved; the customer chooses a hub below 90% within 8 km.
Rule fails (steps 4–5) – e.g. S < 50,000 or COD above 1,000,000: the page explains which rule.
Payment fails (step 6) – ROLLBACK, so no partial order exists (NFR5).
