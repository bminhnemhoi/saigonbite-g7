CLOUD KITCHEN · HO CHI MINH CITY
The idea. A map pin with a bite taken out of it: the nearest kitchen (location, hub load) meets the food. Inside the pin, a steaming bowl – phở, the city’s signature dish.
  • Keep clear space equal to the bite radius around the logo.
  • Use the mark alone at sizes below 120 px wide (app icon, favicon, slide footer).
  • Never recolour the mark; on photos, place it on a dark overlay.
